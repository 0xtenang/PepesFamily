# Security review — 2026-10-29

An internal review, not an audit:
- Eight reviewers each covered one area: accounting, price manipulation, position lifecycle, access and reentrancy, session keys, relayer, web app, keeper.
- Two adversarial verifiers checked every finding: one reproduced it, usually with a Foundry test on an isolated copy, and one judged its real-world impact.
- A completeness critic looked for gaps.

**Result:** 54 findings confirmed, plus 2 from the critic. 6 were disputed (the mechanism is real, but the impact verifier found no practical harm). None was refuted.

Status, 2026-10-29: **53 of 54 confirmed findings fixed, 1 mitigated (F19). The critic's high finding is fixed; its medium one is only partly fixed and still open.** Each contract fix has a Foundry regression test (`contracts/test/Review2Regression.t.sol` and the tests named below). The relayer's fixes are checked by `relayer/src/abuse.ts`, and the keeper and web fixes by their own runs. Retested on a fresh local deployment: 177 offline contract tests, the 15 Robinhood Chain fork tests, the keeper e2e, the relayer e2e (34 checks), and the web checks (`check:chain`, `check:session`, `check:relay`). The full browser flow was also run: wallet open and close, one-click setup, one-click open and close, and logout.

Disputed findings were not changed, except F39: the v1 `CreatorFeeRouter` it concerns has been deleted.
